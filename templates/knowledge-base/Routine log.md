# Routine log

Every routine (morning brief, evening wrap-up, Sunday review, monthly money review) reads this before writing its report, and appends a line for each thing it raised at the end of its run. My answers go in too, in whatever chat I give them.

## Rules
- Don't raise anything logged in the last 7 days again unless something changed: a new reply, a deadline now 2 days away or less, a different amount, or I asked to be reminded.
- Each email gets flagged once, in one place: the first routine that sees it. Later routines leave it out unless it changed.
- An unanswered question is asked once more at most, in the Sunday review, then dropped (or turned into an action in [[Actions]]).
- A TODO suggestion I didn't take up isn't suggested again for 14 days.
- One line each: `YYYY-MM-DD | routine | kind | key | what | status`
  - kind: email, question, todo, money, reminder, action
  - key: short and stable, so the same thing gets the same key next time, e.g. `email:shop/delivery`, `q:card-charge-4nov`, `money:account-short-dec`, `action:A3`
  - status: raised, answered (with the answer), done, declined
- The Sunday review deletes lines older than 30 days. Lasting answers go into the relevant note first.
